🪐🌍 MY PLANET — Bring a World to Life! 🌱🌸

Welcome to My Planet, a peaceful yet challenging world-building game where every seed matters! 🌿✨

🎯 YOUR MAIN MISSION

🌎 Cover at least 40% of the planet with plants!

Transform a cold, lifeless world into a beautiful green paradise filled with flowers 🌸, trees 🌳, butterflies 🦋, bees 🐝, and friendly bears 🐻!

But be careful! ☄️ Asteroids threaten your growing ecosystem. Protect your plants, collect seeds, and help nature survive!

🌱 HOW TO PLAY

👆 Tap a tile to develop life.

🌍 Swipe to rotate the planet and explore its surface.

🔍 Zoom in and out to discover new areas.

☄️ Watch for asteroids! An impact on empty ice can create the first spark of life.

🌿 Expand life to neighboring tiles, then grow flowers and trees.

🌸 GROW YOUR PLANET

🧊 Ice: Create life for 1 seed. Grow a flower for 5 seeds.

🟤 Earth: Create life for 2 seeds. Grow a flower for 10 seeds.

🛡️ Metal: Activate a protective shield for 3 seeds.

🌸 Flowers: Generate seeds automatically. They can grow where ice and earth meet or near metal.

🌳 Trees: Upgrade a flower into a tree for 12 seeds. Trees produce more seeds and can survive one direct asteroid hit.

🐻 Friendly Bears: Invite a bear to a tree for 12 seeds after growing enough trees. Feed hungry bears for 3 seeds to keep them happy and protective!

✨ MAGICAL BONUSES

🌟 Golden Seeds: Tap a golden seed to collect 4 bonus seeds!

🌙 Fireflies: Catch glowing fireflies at night to earn 2 extra seeds!

🦋 Butterflies: Tap a butterfly to temporarily triple seed production from nearby flowers!

🌧️ Rain Clouds: Tap a rain cloud to help flowers bloom. If there are no suitable flowers, receive 3 seeds instead!

☀️🌙 Day and Night: Watch your planet change with time. Flowers produce fewer seeds at night, so plan carefully!

☄️ SURVIVE AND PROTECT

💥 Asteroids can damage your plants and destroy precious life.

🛡️ Metal shields protect neighboring living tiles from asteroid fragments.

🌳 Trees are stronger than flowers.

🐻 Well-fed bears provide additional protection.

💚 If all life disappears from your planet, the game is over!

🏆 CAN YOU SAVE YOUR PLANET?

🌱 Spread life.

🌸 Grow flowers.

🌳 Build forests.

🦋 Attract wildlife.

🛡️ Defend your ecosystem.

🌍 Reach 40% plant coverage and turn your little planet into a living paradise!

💚 Every seed counts. Every flower matters. The future of your planet is in your hands!
